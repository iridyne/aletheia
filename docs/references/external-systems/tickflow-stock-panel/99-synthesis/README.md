# Cross-Domain Synthesis

This directory integrates the twelve completed domain studies into one
TickFlow-to-Aletheia decision model.

## Reports

- [`executive-summary.md`](executive-summary.md) — overall judgment, strongest
  practices, critical cautions, and the combined target.
- [`practice-matrix.md`](practice-matrix.md) — domain-by-domain and pattern-level
  adopt, adapt, preserve, defer, investigate, and reject decisions.
- [`combined-architecture.md`](combined-architecture.md) — the cross-layer
  architecture that combines TickFlow's analytical/UX strengths with
  Aletheia's authority and recovery invariants.
- [`priority-and-experiments.md`](priority-and-experiments.md) — expected-value,
  Alpha relevance, sequencing constraints, and small evidence-bearing
  experiment shapes.
- [`research-reflections.md`](research-reflections.md) — cross-domain insights,
  contradictions, unresolved questions, and lessons about the research method.

## Evidence Boundary

The synthesis is an interpretation of the static evidence already recorded in
the twelve domain folders. It does not add runtime, browser, benchmark, CI,
security-probe, provider, or test-execution evidence. Proposed experiments are
future evidence shapes, not completed validation and not implementation
authorization.

Do not place live implementation tracking here. Do not promote a promising
reference practice into an Aletheia contract or proposal until its owning
governance boundary has been reviewed.
