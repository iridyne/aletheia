# TASK-2026-050 handoff

## Purpose

Extend the opportunity causal chain into trade entry and close decisions. Record and display evidence-backed reasons for entry price, take-profit, stop-loss, account risk/notional, leverage, and close action without changing risk limits, execution semantics, credentials, or live-trading controls.

## Changes made

- Added the implementation plan and a focused contract for trade entry/exit explanations.
- System Operator trades now require five AI reasons before opening: entry price, stop loss,
  take profit, notional, and leverage. The persisted proposal records the final resolved
  values, their real source (snapshot/default/cap), estimated loss at stop, and margin estimate.
- Added `positions.exit_reasoning`; normal protective closes, System Operator portfolio-review
  closes, and verified/administrative reconciliation closures save their real actor, source,
  trigger, and explanation without inventing an AI judgment.
- Extended the existing causal-chain drawer with Entry Reasoning and Close Reasoning nodes;
  order anchors resolve their existing resulting position by `order_id`.
- Fixed the existing Bitget broker averaging test so its fake client also stubs wallet transfer;
  it no longer makes a real transfer request during a unit test.
