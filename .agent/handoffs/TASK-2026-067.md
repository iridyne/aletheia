## Changes made

- Opportunity and ranking work no longer disappears silently: rejection evidence is
  durable, retry work is fair and bounded, and producer time budgets do not let one
  slow candidate starve the rest of the batch.
- System Operator entry authority is the exact durable pending item. A heartbeat cannot
  bypass that queue, dependency failures preserve retryable work, and a crash between
  the final decision and Proposal insert can recover one Proposal instead of leaving a
  permanent `submitting` tombstone.
- Active entry actions are now honest: `trade` or `skip`. Portfolio review is `hold` or
  `close_one`. Unsupported historical actions no longer appear as implemented business
  functions or create hidden side effects.
- Local paper entries require a fresh price for the exact venue, canonical symbol, and
  physical market. The Proposal price must equal that current mark; local limit orders
  are rejected, and every native paper entry carries a complete entry/stop/target/
  reward-risk plan whose geometry is checked before approval.
- Authoritative Risk validates the evaluated Proposal against the stored Proposal across
  every protected field, preserves exact Opportunity and Serenity authority, includes
  unresolved/pending exposure, and prevents concurrent slot, symbol, or notional
  oversubscription.
- Paper execution no longer invents take profit after approval or merges different
  Proposals into one Position. Routed and dashboard closes require fresh exact marks;
  batch close is all-or-zero when any required mark is unavailable, and partial failures
  are reported instead of being presented as success.
- Local-paper futures now have an explicit funding simulator backed by a durable public
  funding schedule. A complete observation window can produce `local_simulation`
  economics; missing schedules fail closed before entry and close-time read failures
  still allow protective exit while keeping Outcome/Learning ineligible.
- Exchange-backed futures remain separate: local simulation is rejected for them, and
  missing venue account-ledger funding keeps their Outcome/Learning path fail-closed.
- Guard, lesson, and prompt influence is path-specific, default-off, and treatment-only;
  control/error cohorts retain the static baseline. Settings exposes the real registry
  state instead of implying an authority that is not active.
- Scheduler and PI health now report actual assessment/debate/TPSL batch failures and
  preserve retryable work instead of turning dependency outages into reassuring green
  state.
- Design contracts and Settings UI/API were updated with the same active behavior.
