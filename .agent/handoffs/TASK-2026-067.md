## Changes made

- Opportunity writers now use atomic database ownership for snapshot and divergence
  deduplication. Concurrent producers cannot create duplicate lifecycle rows or rewrite
  a terminal Opportunity after a stale read.
- Filter/Ranker durably records symbol and eligibility rejection, advances a fair cursor
  over never-seen and retry work, and uses indexed bounded queries. Continuous new input
  no longer starves older retryable Opportunities.
- A new entry must match exactly one durable pending row by source, Opportunity, symbol,
  direction, and market. Serenity also requires an active `pi_evaluate` opportunity under
  a promoted theme. Missing, orphaned, conflicting, or duplicate carriers fail closed.
- Heartbeats can no longer turn an open/ranked analysis row into a Proposal without that
  pending authority. A crash after the final decision claim can recover the same decision
  and create exactly one Proposal instead of leaving it permanently `submitting`.
- Historical entry decisions no longer swallow later Position management. Close and reduce
  use action-scoped idempotency, so each can be recorded once and replayed without requiring
  a new-entry pending signal.
- Authoritative Risk compares every hash-covered Proposal field except its explicitly
  normalized quantity. It counts pending exchange Orders, validates unresolved Order and
  Position identity, and acquires a durable pre-Order reservation in the same transaction
  as approval so concurrent workers cannot oversubscribe a slot, symbol, or notional cap.
- Paper crash replay performs a fresh dynamic preflight when durable authority is absent.
  Local paper no longer invents take profit after approval, and a slipped Order cannot be
  edited downward to hide unresolved exposure.
- Bitget, OKX, and Binance share fail-closed ambiguous-submission handling. Network, 5xx,
  unreadable, or structurally invalid responses remain `submission_unknown` and are not
  retried into a duplicate exchange Order. Explicit valid venue rejections remain known
  failures.
- Paper account writes are serialized by SQLite transaction ownership rather than an
  inverse process lock. Account loss, close-all, Risk, and safety loops inspect the full
  relevant Position/event set; a hidden row beyond a display page cannot bypass safety.
- Close-time attribution read failures are recorded separately from proven missing
  attribution. A durable hook job rechecks the facts, atomically records the final
  eligible/ineligible result, and only then permits Attribution and Learning consumers.
- Post-trade review uses a durable round-robin cursor and bounded database pages: at most
  20 strategies, 200 outcomes per strategy, and 20 model calls per round. Empty, failed,
  and already-reviewed strategies advance fairly without unbounded scans.
- Futures funding remains honest and fail-closed. Wrong-account, simulated exchange, or
  missing funding facts cannot create eligible net PnL, Attribution, or Learning evidence.
