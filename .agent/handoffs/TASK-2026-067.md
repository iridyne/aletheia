## Changes made

- Filter/Ranker records permanent symbol and eligibility rejection, scans the actual
  trade FIFO rather than letting prediction observations bury it, and separates a useful
  ranker preview from entry authority. A new trade is executable only when opportunity,
  source, symbol, direction, and market type exactly match one durable pending row.
- A heartbeat with no pending row can no longer turn an open/ranked opportunity into a
  Proposal or leave an anonymous final decision. The Operator still exposes that row for
  analysis, but returns an explicit `pending_signal_required` result and leaves it open.
- The Operator's close/reduce authority and every safety loop now see the complete open
  Position set. Display and prompt lists remain bounded and say how many rows are hidden;
  an older Position cannot disappear from close, price, stop, reconciliation, or Risk
  authority because it is beyond a default 50/100-row page.
- Divergence Opportunities are deduplicated by a stable source observation. A bare
  ticker proves spot only; bearish observations without a proven perpetual instrument
  are skipped instead of being silently relabelled as shortable futures.
- Serenity promotion is one transaction per theme. Every active opportunity receives
  an exact pending carrier before the theme becomes promoted; an identity mismatch or
  insert failure rolls the whole theme back and leaves it retryable. Manual and automatic
  promotion use the same production helper.
- Risk counts pending exchange Orders and now also acquires one durable pre-Order
  reservation in the same `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction as approval. Concurrent workers
  cannot both consume the last slot/symbol/notional allowance before either Order exists.
  The reservation remains after an ambiguous crash and is released only after an Order
  or a known terminal no-Order state becomes durable.
- Authoritative Risk still verifies every hash-covered Proposal field except its
  explicitly normalized quantity. Local paper execution cannot invent take profit after
  approval, and native stop/take-profit authority remains the immutable Proposal rather
  than mutable configuration.
- Dashboard approval safely resumes real `approved`/`executing` crash residue through
  the idempotent Gateway and produces one Order. Human rejection is allowed only before
  an Order/execution claim exists, atomically releases its Risk reservation, and cannot
  pretend a submitted venue Order was cancelled.
- The existing persistent execution lease prevents duplicate Proposal submission. An
  accepted/uncertain entry or close preserves a retry-suppressing unresolved state;
  `overfilled` remains permanent manual reconciliation across Bitget, OKX, and Binance.
- Futures funding provenance is account-scoped. Local simulation is rejected for exchange
  accounts and broker-ledger funding is rejected for local paper. Missing or wrong funding
  can close the execution lifecycle, but cannot create net PnL, local cash settlement,
  Outcome, Attribution, Feedback, thesis verdict, or post-trade review evidence.
- Post-trade review now consumes only centrally eligible closed Outcomes and fingerprints
  their Position identities. Administrative/incomplete closes do not call the model or
  move Kelly sample counts; an honest no-evidence run reports an explicit skipped result.
- Raw account history selects the newest requested window before restoring chronological
  order, so a row limit cannot keep the oldest 10,000 and silently omit recent incidents.
- Two real production-code paper paths now prove the chain: Gateway/Risk/broker through
  routed close, Order/Fill, Outcome, Attribution, outbox, Feedback, and post-trade review,
  all retaining the same causal identities.
