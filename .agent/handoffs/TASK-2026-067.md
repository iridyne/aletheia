## Changes made

- Filter/Ranker now records permanent symbol and eligibility rejections atomically,
  scans a bounded FIFO workset instead of starving older opportunities, and hands only
  durable `signal_pending` identities to the System Operator.
- The Operator can no longer turn an arbitrary open/ranked opportunity into a trade.
  It requires the exact pending carrier, atomically stores the prepared Proposal and
  final decision, and safely recovers the historical claim-before-insert crash gap.
- Historical Serenity pending rows with a missing market type are recoverable only when
  their linked opportunity ID, symbol, direction, and source market type all match.
- Risk now counts pending exchange entries as unresolved exposure and verifies that the
  authoritative evaluated Proposal matches the stored immutable snapshot in every
  hash-covered field except its explicitly normalized quantity.
- Local paper execution no longer invents a take-profit value after Risk approval.
  Order, Fill, Position, cash, and audit materialization remain one durable chain.
- A persistent execution lease prevents two workers or a crash replay from submitting
  one Proposal twice. Exchange results are accepted only when their durable Order,
  Fill, and Position evidence supports the returned state.
- If an exchange has accepted or may have accepted an entry/close, later read, status,
  or secondary audit failures preserve a retry-suppressing unresolved state instead of
  rewriting the Proposal or close as a terminal error. Bitget, OKX, and Binance share
  the same post-durable audit isolation.
- An `overfilled` close is now a permanent manual-reconciliation state. All three
  brokers and the Position manager suppress any automatic resubmission, preserving the
  original close claim and preventing another exchange call.
- Native stop/take-profit authority comes from the immutable Proposal. Configuration
  changes cannot add protection to a native Position whose approved Proposal explicitly
  left it empty; adopted/legacy Positions retain their documented fallback.
- Futures funding is never guessed. When trusted funding economics are absent, close
  lifecycle state can complete but realized PnL, paper cash authority, Outcome learning,
  exports, notifications, and UI judgement remain explicitly unavailable.
- Account and portfolio completeness checks inspect the full relevant history, so an
  old incomplete futures close cannot disappear behind a row limit and silently restore
  trading authority.
- The dashboard now renders unavailable PnL/funding as unavailable rather than `$0`,
  green, or a zero-width success bar.
- A real local-paper round-trip test now enters through the production Gateway/Risk/broker,
  closes through the production router, and proves the close Order/Fill, Outcome,
  Attribution, outbox, and feedback keep one causal identity.
